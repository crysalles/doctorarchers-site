Privacy

How I handle your privacy

The short version: your information is used only for what you asked for, and it is never sold.

Your email

  • If you share your email (for the free chapter, the newsletter, or to get in touch), it is used only to send you what you requested.
  • Your email address is never sold or shared with third parties for their marketing.
  • Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribing is honored immediately.

This website

  • This site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or trackers. Every page is served from this domain only.
  • The site itself sets no cookies.
  • When you follow a link to an outside service (booking via OnceHub, labs via Rupa Health, supplements via Fullscript, or the book checkout), that service's own privacy policy applies from that point on.

Client information

Anything you share with LaVeena, or within the Private Membership Association, is treated as confidential.

Who runs this site

LaVeenaArchers.com is operated by ICONIC Human Design LLC (also operating as ICONIC Human Design Education PMA and related brands), based in the United States.

What we collect and why

We collect only what you choose to give us, such as your name and email when you request the free chapter, subscribe, use the contact form, or make a purchase, along with the basic technical data your browser sends. Payment details are handled by third-party processors; we never store full card numbers. We use your information to deliver what you asked for, provide support, and send updates you opted into.

Sharing, retention, and your rights

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help run this business (for example email, hosting, and payment processors), and where required by law. Records are kept as long as needed for the purpose they were collected and for legal and accounting requirements, typically five years or more. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time, and readers in the EU/EEA and California have the additional rights granted under the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA. This site is not directed to children under 16.

Contact and unsubscribe

For any privacy request, or to ask how your information is handled, use the contact form. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any message.
