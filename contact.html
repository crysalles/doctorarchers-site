Scope of practice. Rev. Dr. LaVeena B. She is not a medical doctor. Her role is to educate and support, not to diagnose, treat, prescribe, or manage diseases. Services are offered within a Private Membership Association (PMA). Nothing on this website is medical advice, and nothing here promises to cure, reverse, or treat any disease.
