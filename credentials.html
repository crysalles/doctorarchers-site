Professional Education & Credentials

Health Credentials

Rev. Dr. LaVeena B. Archers, PhD

My training sits in natural medicine, functional nutrition, and holistic practice, alongside a separate and much longer education in Human Design. Below is every degree, board certification, and training certificate I hold, with the institution that issued it and a plain statement of what it is and what it is not. The certificates themselves carry the dates.

I would rather you see the whole picture than a list of letters after a name. Nothing here is a state license to practice medicine, and I have said so next to each credential rather than in small print at the bottom.

Board certifications

Awarded in 2025 by the American Natural Wellness Practitioners Board.

What these are, and are not. Private board certification issued by the American Natural Wellness Practitioners Board (ANWPB), a private credentialing organization whose certification is not state or federally regulated. This is not a state medical license and does not authorize the practice of medicine or naturopathic medicine in Arizona.

Kingdom College of Natural Health

About this institution. Issued by Kingdom College of Natural Health, which is not accredited by an agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. This degree is in natural health, not medicine, and confers no license to practice medicine or naturopathic medicine in any state.

Rockwell School of Holistic Medicine

Five Doctor of Philosophy titles, granted across 2025. Together these make up the pentadoctorate, and they are the source of the PhD in my name.

About this institution. Issued by Rockwell School of Holistic Medicine, which is not accredited by an agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. These are titles in holistic and natural medicine. None of them is a state medical license, and none authorizes the practice of medicine or naturopathic medicine in Arizona.

Rockwell certificates of completion

Additional certificates and coaching credentials

Independent training programs, each with its own issuing body. These are certificates of completion in a specific skill, not degrees and not licenses.

Human Design training

A separate discipline from the health credentials above, studied from 2012 onward, largely through the International Human Design School and the BG5 Career & Business Institute.

What this training is, and is not. Human Design is a self-knowledge system, not medicine. These are private courses from independent Human Design schools. They are not academic degrees, they are not accredited by an agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education, and they are not health-care or state credentials. Nothing in this training diagnoses, treats, or is a substitute for medical care.

What all of this means in practice

These credentials support educational, coaching, and holistic wellness services. They do not constitute state licensure to practice medicine, naturopathic medicine, or any regulated health profession in Arizona. Services are offered within a private educational and coaching framework.

I am not a medical doctor. I do not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or manage disease, I do not run a clinical practice, and I am not licensed to practice medicine or naturopathic medicine in any state. My role is to educate and support, alongside your own medical care and never instead of it.

Two of the institutions above, Rockwell School of Holistic Medicine and Kingdom College of Natural Health, are not accredited by an agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. The American Natural Wellness Practitioners Board is a private credentialing organization, and its certification is not state or federally regulated. I would rather tell you that plainly here than have you find it out somewhere else.
