# Disallow stops CRAWLING. It does not stop INDEXING, and the two get confused
# constantly. A page that is disallowed but linked from elsewhere can still be
# listed in search results — as a bare URL, because the crawler was never
# allowed to fetch the page and read the noindex telling it to stay out. So for
# anything that carries a noindex, the disallow is worse than useless: it is the
# thing preventing the noindex from being obeyed. Disallow is only right for
# content that should never be fetched at all.
