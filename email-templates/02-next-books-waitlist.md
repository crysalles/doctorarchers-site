# Email 2 — The next books (waitlist)

**Send:** about 14 days after someone joins the reader list.
**Goal:** turn a reader into a day-one buyer for books two and three, and learn
which one they actually want.

---

## Subject line options

1. What I am writing next (and a question for you)
2. Two more books are coming. Which one do you need first?
3. The next one is about GLP-1

**Preview text:** Bad Medicine Blues was the first of a series. Here is what comes after it.

---

## Body

Hi {{ subscriber.first_name | default: "there" }},

*Bad Medicine Blues* was never meant to stand on its own. It is the first of a
series — the Root-Cause Guides — and two more are already taking shape.

**Your Body's Own GLP-1** *(in progress)*

If the weight-loss injections have you curious, or uneasy, or both, this is the
complete picture. What the drugs actually do, what they cost you, and every
natural way to support the GLP-1 your body already makes. I am trying to write
the fair version — the one that is honest about what the medicine achieves and
equally honest about the limits of the natural path. Most of what is out there
picks a side. I would rather tell you the truth.

**The Good Years** *(planned)*

A grounded guide to aging well, built on the same root-cause foundations.
Living better for longer, rather than chasing whatever is being sold this year.

You do not need to sign up for anything. You are already on the reader list,
which is the waitlist — you will hear about each book before anyone else, and
there is nothing else to keep track of.

But I do have a question, and I read every reply.

**Which of those two do you need more, and what do you most want answered in
it?**

Just hit reply and tell me. Not a survey, not a form. I am genuinely writing
these books for the woman reading this email, and the more specifically I know
what you are stuck on, the better I can write them.

Thank you for being here,

Rev. Dr. LaVeena B. Archers, PhD

P.S. If you have read *Bad Medicine Blues* and have not left a review yet, it
would still help enormously. Two honest sentences is plenty:
[leave one here](https://www.amazon.com/review/create-review?asin=B0H9JKKSRT).

---

## Notes

- **The reply question is the most valuable line in this email.** Replies teach
  Kit's sending reputation that people engage with you, and they hand you the
  exact language your readers use, which is what the next book's chapters and
  the site's articles should be written in. Keep every reply.
- Reuse this email whenever a book hits a milestone (first draft done, cover
  reveal, pre-order). The waitlist is only worth having if it hears from you.
- Keep the framing educational. Describe what the books explain, never what
  they will fix.
