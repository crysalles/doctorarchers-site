The Good Years Method

The whole book in miniature. Four foundations you build on, a lens you lay over them because you are a woman, and one filter you run everything else through.

  • Move and Build. Keep your muscle, strength, fitness, and balance. The single most powerful lever, at every age.
  • Nourish. Eat whole food, tend your gut, steady your blood sugar, and be honest about what you drink.
  • Restore. Protect your sleep, calm your nervous system, and care for your mind.
  • Belong. Stay woven into people, hold onto your purpose, keep connection alive.
  • Attune, the female lens. Learn and honor your body's turning points, from perimenopause onward, and act within their window.
  • Filter, the honest test. Run every longevity promise through one question. Is this proven, or is someone selling it to me?

Your two-minute self-check

Read each foundation and count how many statements are true for you right now. The one where you score lowest is the best place to begin.

Move and Build

I do strength training at least twice a week. I move at an easy pace most days. I practice my balance. I feel strong enough for the demands of my daily life.

Nourish

Most of what I eat is whole, real food. I get real protein at every meal. I eat a wide variety of plants across a week. My drinking is genuinely light, or none.

Restore

I sleep well most nights. I have real ways to calm my stress. My mood is steady, and I would reach for help if it were not.

Belong

I have people I feel truly close to. I have a reason to get up in the morning. I feel connected rather than isolated.

Attune

I understand where I am in the menopause transition. I know my key numbers, like blood pressure and blood sugar. I have a clinician who takes my body seriously.

Give yourself one point for each true statement. The foundation where you scored lowest is where your next good years are most easily won. Pick one thing there, begin it this week, and let it become ordinary before you add the next.

A longevity day

  • Get outdoor light early, and move your body in some way before the day gets busy.
  • Eat real food at each meal, with a genuine source of protein every time and plenty of plants.
  • Take a short walk after meals when you can, to help steady your blood sugar.
  • Do your strength work or your easy cardio, whichever the day calls for.
  • Build in a little calm, some nature, and a real moment of connection with someone you care about.
  • Wind down as the evening comes, dim the screens, and give yourself the best chance at deep sleep.

Start today

If you do one thing today, make it this. Stand up from a chair without using your hands, ten times, and take a brisk ten-minute walk. Muscle and movement are the single most powerful lever there is, at every age, and they cost nothing.
