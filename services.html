Health Assessments & Analyses

Basic Health Overview

A first look at where your health stands. Together we review your history, your symptoms, and your goals, and map the foundations most worth your attention, so you leave with a clear, doable starting point rather than a pile of tests.

Foundational Health Analysis

A deeper, whole-person look at the systems that shape how you feel day to day, your energy, digestion, stress, sleep, and nutrition, to find the root patterns beneath the symptoms and where the highest-value changes lie.

Advanced Health Analysis

For complex or long-standing concerns. A comprehensive functional review that can include interpreting lab work you bring, connecting the threads across your whole story to find what earlier, single-issue visits missed.

Recorded or Live Sessions

Meet live by video, or receive a recorded analysis and walkthrough you can revisit any time. The depth is the same either way, and distance is never a barrier.
