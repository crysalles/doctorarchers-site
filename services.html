Services

Ways to work together

Education and support, blending traditional healing, modern functional insights, and holistic lifestyle strategies, informed by Human Design. All services are offered to members within a Private Membership Association. Package details are shared during your consultation, so we can match the right depth to your goals.

Health Education

Basic Health Overview

A first look at the foundations of your health. Together we walk through your history and your goals, and map the foundations most worth learning about, so you leave with a clear, doable starting point rather than a pile of tests.

Foundational Health Education

A deeper, whole-person look at how the systems that shape how you feel day to day fit together, your energy, digestion, stress, sleep, and nutrition, so you can see the root patterns beneath the symptoms and where the highest-value changes lie.

Advanced Health Education

For complex or long-standing questions. A deeper educational session where we connect the threads across your whole story and you learn to read your own lab work, so you can have a better-informed conversation with your own provider.

Recorded or Live Sessions

Meet live by video, or receive a recorded session and walkthrough you can revisit any time. The depth is the same either way, and distance is never a barrier.

Wellness Coaching & Programs

Foundational Wellness Program

A guided start on the free, powerful foundations, food, light, sleep, movement, and stress, built around your life and your Human Design so the changes actually fit and actually last.

Holistic Health Coaching Packages

Ongoing, personalized support as you rebuild your health step by step, with education, accountability, and gentle course-corrections along the way rather than a rigid one-size plan.

Advanced Transformation Programs

For those ready to go all in. A deeper, longer engagement that weaves the physical, the nutritional, and the mind-body-spirit work into one coherent path toward lasting change.

Career & Wellness Consulting

For entrepreneurs, executives, partnerships, and teams.

Wellness Consultation

A focused session to bring your health and your work back into alignment, so your body can keep up with your ambition instead of paying the price for it.

Partnership Health Education

For couples and business partners. Learn how each of you is wired, through Human Design and a whole-person health lens, so you can support rather than drain one another.

Full Wellness Coaching Package

Comprehensive, ongoing coaching for the high-output life, combining the health foundations with the strategy and accountability to keep them in place through a demanding schedule.

Executive Health Mentorship

Private, high-touch mentorship for leaders who cannot afford to burn out, protecting your energy, focus, and longevity as deliberately as you protect your business.

Team Wellness Engineering

Bring holistic, root-cause wellness to your whole team, with education and simple systems that lift energy, focus, and resilience across the organization.
