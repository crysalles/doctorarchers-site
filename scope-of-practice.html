Scope of Practice

Holistic health and wellness teacher

As a Certified Executive Coach, Doctor of Natural Medicine (DNM), Board-Certified Holistic Functional Medicine Doctor (BC-HFMD) and Holistic Functional Nutrition Doctor (BC-HFND), LaVeena blends traditional healing, modern functional insight, and holistic lifestyle strategy to help members reach vibrant, aligned well-being, longevity, and healthspan.

Her approach

LaVeena's approach is centered on education, support, and personalization. Her role is to educate and support, not to diagnose, treat, prescribe, or manage diseases. She works within the private domain of a Private Membership Association, a consensual space for mutual growth.

As a Doctor of Natural Medicine (DNM)

She guides members in exploring natural and integrative approaches that honor the body's own healing potential. Working from a whole-person perspective, she attends to physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual wellness, without diagnosing, treating, or managing diseases. This includes:

  • Teaching holistic health principles and how to build your own wellness routine
  • Nutrition education and dietary approaches (for example keto, AIP, paleo)
  • Education on herbal remedies, homeopathy, and natural supplementation
  • Emotional and energetic alignment through mind-body practices
  • Gentle detoxification, immune support, and approaches to chronic wellness
  • Frequency medicine, sound healing, and other vibrational therapies

As a Board-Certified Holistic Functional Medicine and Nutrition Doctor

In her functional medicine work, she helps members uncover underlying imbalances through a systems-biology lens, focusing on restoring cellular and metabolic harmony to support energy, mood, and resilience, without diagnosing, treating, or managing diseases. She assists members in:

  • Teaching members to understand their own functional lab results for thyroid, hormones, gut health, nutrients, and more
  • Understanding their own biochemistry and building their own wellness routines
  • Learning about mitochondrial function, insulin sensitivity, and immune balance
  • Learning natural approaches to metabolism, digestion, and hormone balance
  • Making sustainable nutritional and lifestyle adjustments for lasting vitality
  • Exploring the connections between physiology and personal purpose

As a Board-Certified Holistic Health Practitioner (BC-HHP)

She empowers members through education and self-discovery, honoring the body's inherent wisdom and encouraging inner healing, without diagnosing, treating, or managing diseases. Her teaching covers:

  • Lifestyle coaching and spiritual wellness support
  • Self-care techniques for energetic and emotional equilibrium
  • Non-invasive tools such as frequency, light, and sound therapies
  • Mind-body-spirit strategies for whole-life transformation
