Your Body’s Own GLP-1 Is Here, in Every Format

July 29, 2026

Written by Rev. Dr. LaVeena B. Archers, PhD
Doctor of Natural Medicine · Board-certified in Holistic Functional Medicine and Functional Nutrition

My new book is out. Your Body’s Own GLP-1 is now on Amazon in Kindle, paperback, and hardcover, and free to read for anyone with Kindle Unlimited.

If the weight-loss shots have you curious, hopeful, or a little worried, this is the book I wrote for you. Not for the medicine, and not against it. A calm, well-researched map of the whole landscape, written for women, so you can see what the drugs actually do, what they cost, and every natural way to support the GLP-1 your own body already makes.

What is inside

  • How GLP-1 works in your body, in plain language
  • What the medicines truly deliver and what they cost, from the real clinical trials
  • The muscle-and-bone problem that matters most for women in midlife
  • The truth about the supplements sold as natural stand-ins
  • Food, gut, movement, and sleep habits that support your own GLP-1
  • A clear framework for deciding, with your prescriber, whether a medicine belongs in your plan

Twenty-one chapters across five parts, plus a free printable Support Kit to take into the room with you.

Who it is for

Women who are tired of advice that contradicts itself by lunchtime, and who want the whole picture in proportion rather than the loudest voice. Whether you are considering a medicine, already on one, or looking for every way to support your body without one, it meets you where you are.

Your body already makes the hormone these drugs imitate. This book is about understanding that, and tending it well.
